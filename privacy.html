Stretchy: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Stretchy does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your best stretch score is saved only on your own device and never leaves it. The app works fully offline.

The app uses your phone's vibration for haptic feedback. It does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.
